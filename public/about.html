ABOUT MAXDAY

A moment in Scripture.

MaxDay shares Bible passages, short original reflections, and prayers to bring a little encouragement to your day. Our Scripture cards are free to keep, print, and share for personal use.

MaxDay is an independent resource. We are not affiliated with a church, Bible publisher, or the services linked below.

About the words you read

Scripture quotations come from the World English Bible, a public-domain English translation. Biblical quotations and their references are identified separately from our original reflections and prayers.

The reflection currently available is based on Matthew 11:28–30. “For today” means a reading you can use today; this page does not promise a newly published reflection every day.

About the card

The olive-grove illustration was created with an image-generation tool. It is an illustration, not a photograph of a biblical location.
