PRIVACY

A simple, free reading.

Last updated September 29, 2026.

This site does not ask for your name, email address, prayer requests, or an account. You can read and get the Scripture card without providing those details.

Website analytics

We use Umami Cloud to understand how visitors reach the page and whether they request a resource. Analytics may include pages visited, referral or campaign information, general location, browser and device information, and actions such as requesting a card or opening the surrounding passage.

Our event tracking does not send names, email addresses, prayer text, or responses about your beliefs. A resource request does not tell us whether you saved, read, printed, or shared the card.

Storage and downloads

We use your browser’s session storage to avoid counting repeated action clicks in the same tab session. We do not use that storage to create an account or save personal reflections. The site still works if analytics or browser storage is unavailable.

Downloading or opening the card requests a static file from our website host, GitHub Pages. The host and analytics provider operate their own infrastructure and privacy practices.

Sharing and external links

Sharing opens your device’s sharing options or copies a link after you choose the share button. We do not receive your contacts or the contents of your message. Links to other websites are subject to those websites’ privacy practices.
